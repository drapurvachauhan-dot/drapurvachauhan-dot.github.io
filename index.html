01 — About

Academic with a
Market Mindset

I am Dr. Apurva A. Chauhan, an Associate Professor of Finance at Manav Rachna International Institute of Research & Studies (MRIIRS), Faridabad. With over a decade and a half of teaching and research experience, I bring the worlds of financial markets and management education together.

My research spans behavioral finance, security analysis, investment decision-making, and capital markets. I have published in Scopus-indexed journals and presented at premier conferences including the India Finance Conference at IIM Ahmedabad.

As a UGC-NET & GSET qualified academic, I have taught a wide spectrum of finance and management subjects at both undergraduate and postgraduate levels across multiple institutions.

Outside research, I serve as a reviewer for several international journals and am an active member of the All India Management Association and Indian Economic Association.

Research & Teaching
Domains

Security Analysis & Portfolio Management
Deep expertise in equity analysis, single index model, portfolio construction, and mutual fund performance evaluation using quantitative methods.
🧠
Behavioral Finance
Researching investor psychology, cognitive biases, celebrity endorsement effects, and non-rational investment decision-making paradigms.
🌐
International Financial Markets
Studying co-integration between global indices (US–India), treasury management, forex markets, and cross-border financial interdependencies.
📉
Corporate Governance & Finance

💡
Sustainable & Ethical Finance
Exploring ethical investment, ESG considerations, digital media influence on investment decisions, and sustainable development financing.
🤖
AI & Data in Finance
Examining the role of artificial intelligence in financial decision-making and leveraging data analytics tools for research insights.
